PollyPet Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

PollyPet is an app that turns photos of your pet into new images. This policy explains what information PollyPet handles, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

PollyPet is provided by [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME] (“we”, “us”), based in Quezon City, Philippines. You can contact us at support@pollypet.app.

Summary

  • PollyPet does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and you don’t need an account to use it.
  • When you choose to transform a photo, that photo is sent to our server and then to OpenAI, which creates the new image. This only happens after you agree in the app.
  • Location and other details stored in the photo are removed before it is sent.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information we handle

Photos you choose to transform

When you tap Generate, PollyPet uploads the photo you selected to our server, which sends it to OpenAI to create the transformed image. We only receive photos you choose to transform. PollyPet does not access the rest of your photo library.

Before upload, the app removes location and other information stored inside the photo file (such as camera details).

Images created for you

The transformed image is stored on our server so the app can download it, and a copy is kept in the app on your device in your History.

App identity

When PollyPet is first opened, the app creates a random identifier for your installation and a secret that only your device knows. Our server stores the identifier and a one-way hash of the secret, and issues an access token so the app can use our service. These are not linked to your name, Apple ID, email address or phone number.

Credits and purchases

We record your credit balances and how each credit was added or used, including free credits and refunds for failed transformations. When you buy credits, Apple processes the payment. We receive a signed record of the transaction from Apple, including the transaction ID and product, so we can add your credits. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.

Messages you send us

How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • create the images you ask for and deliver them to you;
  • keep track of your credits and apply purchases correctly;
  • prevent abuse, such as automated attempts to collect free credits;
  • respond to your support requests;
  • keep the service working and fix problems.

We do not use your photos or images for advertising. [VERIFY: we do not use photos to train any AI model.]

  • OpenAI, which generates the transformed images. It receives the photo you chose to transform and instructions describing the transformation.   • [HOSTING AND STORAGE PROVIDERS], which run our server and store data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

These service providers process information to provide their services to us. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

PollyPet asks for your permission before it sends any photo to OpenAI. If you don’t agree, no photo is sent, but you won’t be able to create transformations.

You can withdraw your permission at any time in the app’s Settings. After that, no further photos are sent. Withdrawing doesn’t affect photos already processed.

You can delete the information we hold about your installation from the app, in Settings > Delete My Data. Because PollyPet has no accounts, the request is made from the app itself, which proves it comes from your installation. If you no longer have the app, we cannot confirm that a request comes from you, because there is no account to check. Information about that installation is then deleted at the end of the periods listed above. You can still contact us at support@pollypet.app with questions.

The support ID shown in Settings helps us find your records when you contact us. It identifies your installation but does not prove a request comes from you, so we do not delete data on the strength of a support ID alone.

Security

We protect information in transit with encryption, store secrets and access tokens only as one-way hashes, and limit access to our systems. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

We are based in the Philippines. Our service providers, including OpenAI, may process information in other countries, such as the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects how photos are shared, the app will ask for your permission again.
